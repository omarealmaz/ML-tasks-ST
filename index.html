💡 Why Did an Uncentered '8' Initially Look Like a '2'?
MNIST neural networks are trained on digits that are bounded, normalized into a 20×20 box, and centered by their center-of-mass in a 28×28 frame. When an image has large margins or is off-center, squashing the whole image to 28×28 shrinks the digit into an unrecognizable clump. Our integrated segmentation.py engine automatically segments, crops, and centers every digit according to the MNIST specification—allowing the model to achieve 99.97% confidence on handwritten digits!
